We reimagine the bathroom through technology and design

Unique and personalized environments, bathrooms with large-format cladding and flooring featuring minimal joints. Next-generation sustainable surfaces with a wide portfolio of perfectly balanced colors and textures, which also shape sinks and shower trays with a meticulously minimalist design.

We offer an extensive range of innovative bathroom solutions with leading brands such as Silestone and ĒCLOS, two different mineral surfaces with reduced silica content; Dekton, an ultra-compact surface; and Sensa, a natural stone with an exclusive stain-resistant protection.

Surfaces for a better world

At Cosentino, we strive to provide the highest quality surfaces, sinks, and shower trays while contributing to building a more sustainable future for all. To achieve this, we blend cutting-edge technological innovation with superior design through a manufacturing process that prioritizes environmental care.

We integrate recycled components, exclusively use renewable electricity, and recover 99% of the water used, thus reducing our environmental footprint. We also reduce and neutralize our CO2 emissions to ensure our impact is always environmentally positive.

A bathroom that sets trends

Through captivating veined designs, refined colors, and sophisticated finishes, our creations and cutting-edge textures blend the latest trends in design, architecture, and interior design with exceptional durability and longevity, specifically crafted to withstand intensive bathroom use.

The large formats of Silestone and ĒCLOS (up to 128x63 inches), Dekton (up to 130x64 inches), and Sensa (starting from 110x71 inches) enable the design of any bathroom without restrictions, creating greater visual harmony to enhance the aesthetics of countertops, cladding, flooring, shower trays, and sinks while simplifying daily care.

Total integration. A modern and harmonious bathroom

The high resistance to stains, scratches, and wear of our surfaces, combined with our extensive experience in producing large-scale pieces and multiple thicknesses, enables us to offer a wide range of possibilities for bathroom design.

This enables the creation of integrated and harmonious environments, where surfaces extend from the countertop to wall cladding, furniture, sinks, shower trays, or floors. The result is a seamless and refined aesthetic that transcends the traditional bathroom concept.

Your bathroom, perfect for longer

Our greatest satisfaction is our customers' trust. Therefore, we create durable surfaces with great visual value capable of remaining intact for generations. We are so confident in our quality that brands like Silestone, ĒCLOS or Dekton come with a 25-year commercial guarantee.

Additionally, for any questions regarding care or potential issues with Cosentino products, you will always have access to a dedicated team of specialists ready to assist you anywhere. Check all details in the Maintenance and Warranty section for each of our brands.
