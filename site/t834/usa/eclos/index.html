A new layer of matter & design

The next generation of mineral surfaces featuring a layered 3D body integrated design and superior heat resistance.

Thanks to its INLAYR® technology, its production combines minerals and recycled materials with a low-crystalline silica composition, through an advanced process of robotic engineering and integrated decorative techniques.

Our debut collection, Eclectic Veins, is formulated with zero crystalline silica*. The result is a high-performance material with realistic aesthetics, and a natural touch & feel, for indoor applications.

A new design landscape.

ĒCLOS features

INLAYR® 3D Body Design Technology

Unlike superficial patterns, ĒCLOS' multi-dimensional approach is achieved through combining a tailored raw material composition with robotics and decoration techniques. The result is a layered 3D integration that adds depth and consistency.

Superior Heat Resistance

We’ve designed ĒCLOS to withstand temperatures of up to 428° F, including direct contact with cookware immediately after use. Hot cookware can be placed directly onto the surface, straight from the stove or oven, without concern for marks or damage.

Zero Silica & Recycled Core

Our debut collection, Eclectic Veins, is formulated with zero crystalline silica*. Made with up to 88% recycled materials and selected minerals.

For Indoor Applications

ĒCLOS is designed exclusively for indoor applications, where its aesthetics and performance are maintained in optimal conditions.

Design Technology

Using advanced decoration techniques and integrated processes, each surface becomes a composition of color, texture, and light, built not in one step, but in layers, for a richer, detailed and more authentic appearance.

INLAYR® 3D Integration

Realistic Details and 3D Integrated Design. Edge to Edge

With INLAYR® design technology, ĒCLOS achieves breathtaking shades & gradient effects that seamlessly blend into a refined composition, delivering a stunning interplay of texture and color. The INLAYR® technology generates delicate and intricate detailed veins that make ĒCLOS surfaces gain depth and sophistication.

With an advanced automation process and special raw materials, INLAYR® design technology enables genuine 3D integration, bringing textures and patterns to life, with depth, and unique personality.

LAYERS OF PERFORMANCE

Designed to Lead. Engineered to Perform

Realistic Aesthetics. A Natural Touch & Feel

ĒCLOS has a natural touch and feel and provides a tactile experience full of depth that feels authentic and organic, inspired by stones and textures found in nature.

Superior Heat Resistance. Up to 428°F

ĒCLOS is designed to withstand temperatures of up to 428°F, including direct contact with cookware immediately after use, without worrying about marks or damage.

Engineered to Perform

ĒCLOS’ advanced composition delivers high-performance and durability against impacts, scratches, stains, and daily use, with easy, low-maintenance care. Its advanced formulation offers high resistance to the daily wear that comes with real-world living. ĒCLOS is crafted to endure through time.

LAYERS OF MATTER

ĒCLOS Redefines Surfaces with Recycled Content and Zero Silica

Zero Silica. Designed from Less

ĒCLOS’ debut collection, Eclectic Veins, is formulated with zero crystalline silica*. It brings together carefully designed patterns with a more thoughtful material composition.

A Mineral Surface. Recycled Core Within

ĒCLOS parts from up to 88% recycled content in its core to bring to life a durable, beautiful mineral surface, creating a purpose-driven, sustainable material from the inside out.

A Process Powered by Nature

ĒCLOS production uses 100% renewable energy and 99% recycled water, reflecting a commitment to sustainability without compromising quality, design, or responsible material sourcing.
