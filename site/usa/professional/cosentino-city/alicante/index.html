Discover everything Cosentino City Alicante has to offer

Located by the sea and the bustling Avenida Maisonnave, Cosentino City Alicante offers a materials library for inspiration and to develop all kinds of projects, several social areas to interact with clients and other professionals, and a zone with advanced digital design tools.

In a 5,382 SQFT space and with a packed agenda of events, talks, and showcookings, Cosentino City Alicante is designed as a meeting point for the creative community and a space to discover the latest Cosentino innovations in various applications, including countertops, facades, flooring, cladding, and bathroom solutions, among others.

Frequently asked questions

01.

What is a Cosentino City?

A Cosentino City is an inspiration and exhibition space designed for architecture and design professionals, as well as end customers. Here, you can discover our innovative Dekton, Silestone, and Sensa surfaces, real applications of our materials in bathrooms, kitchens, and other spaces, and receive personalized advice from our experts.
02.

Do I need to make an appointment to visit a Cosentino City?

We recommend scheduling an appointment to ensure personalized and dedicated service. This way, we can guarantee one of our experts will be available to show you around, answer all your questions, and help you find the perfect solution for your project.
03.

What will I find during my visit to a Cosentino City?

At a Cosentino City, you will have access to a wide range of samples of our products in various colors, textures, and finishes. You can view large-format slabs to appreciate the beauty and veining of the materials in full scale. Additionally, you'll find different settings and applications to inspire your projects, ranging from bathroom elements, facades, and flooring to kitchen countertops.
04.

Are events or workshops organized at Cosentino City?

Yes, Cosentino Cities are hubs for the architecture and design community. We regularly organize events, new product presentations, training workshops, and networking sessions for our network of professionals. We recommend checking the social media channels of the Cosentino City of your interest to stay updated on upcoming events.
05.

Can I purchase products directly at a Cosentino City?

At Cosentino City, we offer our products and advice to both professionals and individual customers. In this space, you can purchase our materials and applications, as well as connect with a trusted network of fabricators and installers who will help you bring your project to life.
