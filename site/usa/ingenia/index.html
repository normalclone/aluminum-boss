What is the Cosentino Ingenia programme?

Cosentino launches an exclusive initiative aimed at attracting and developing professionals with a STEM (Science, Technology, Engineering and Mathematics) education and a passion for the industrial sector.

As part of its commitment to fostering the professional growth of versatile individuals with great potential, Cosentino offers this career acceleration programme to enable participants to take on positions of responsibility:

Production Shift Manager
Production Engineer
Industrial Maintenance Engineer
Service Engineer/Specialist
Continuous Improvement Engineer
Process Quality Engineer

What are we looking for?

To be eligible for the programme, you must meet the following requirements:

Have completed your studies in the above fields.

Have less than two years of work experience.

Have a good command of English.

Immediate availability to start the programme.

Live in the area or be willing to relocate to the area.

Willingness to work shifts in the positions that require it.

What can the Cosentino Ingenia programme offer you?

At Cosentino, we want to give you the tools to become the professional you want to be. We offer you:

A permanent contract with a competitive salary from day one.

A training plan that includes certification in Lean Manufacturing and technical training in production processes, as well as the development of communication, team management and leadership skills.

Rotation in different positions within the department.

Attendance at events and conferences with relevant industry professionals.

Involvement in challenging projects with high business impact.

Using the latest production technology in a multinational company that is constantly growing and offering many opportunities for professional development.
