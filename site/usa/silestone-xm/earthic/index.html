EARTHIC IS MORE THAN JUST A SURFACE

EARTHIC IS NOT JUST A PRODUCT, IT IS A COSENTINO RESEARCH PROJECT ON MATERIALS AND SUSTAINABILITY IN COLLABORATION WITH THE ICONIC STUDIO FORMAFANTASMA.

Earthic is a sustainable surface with a maximum of 10% silica, created with an innovative composition and complex production process that is the product of years of R&D. It utilizes a selection of premium minerals, recycled glass, PET, bioresin, and Dekton fragments to reduce the extraction of raw materials, limiting soil erosion and CO2 emissions. Additionally, it’s manufactured with 99% recycled water and 100% certified renewable electric energy.

Earthic incorporates up to 30% recycled components into its eco-conscious surfaces.

EARTHIC X FORMAFANTASMA

EARTHIC X FORMAFANTASMA

Formafantasma is a research-based design studio investigating the ecological, historical, political and social forces shaping the design discipline today.

Cosentino has partnered with design visionaries, Formafantasma, to craft a new collection that explores innovative and sustainable material sources.

Formafantasma brings forward a new approach to surface design.

FFROM THE INSIDE OUT

We have carefully examined our production processes to integrate waste and recycled materials reutilization. These are then externally applied to Earthic surfaces.

Recycled Dekton Fragments 

WE MINIMIZE WASTE. WE REUSE

We incorporate sustainable material sourcing by recycling the discarded Dekton from our production process, which accounts for approximately 0.125% of its weight.

Recycled Oil Bio-Resin 

FFROM THE KITCHEN TO YOUR KITCHEN

Earthic incorporates a variable content of 16% to 23% post-consumer bio-resin into its total resin composition. This bio-resin, derived from vegetable oil and recycled cooking oil, transforms components that would otherwise be discarded. Meet the bio-resin fueled surface.

Recycled Glass 

A BRIGHT FUTURE FOR GLASS

Earthic uses post-industrial recycled glass in its design, avoiding the extraction of new raw materials, preventing soil erosion, and reducing CO2 emissions. This component becomes an integral part of Earthic’s surface, shaping its design from within.

Recycled PET 

RETHINKING SINGLE-USE PLASTIC

By utilizing recycled PET, Earthic reduces CO2 emissions and greenhouse gases. Through an innovative process, plastic bottles are transformed into distinctive scales that reveal their unique characteristics on the surface. This way, recycled PET remains useful for years.

Selected Minerals Q10 

LESS SILICA

Earthic is the result of a new combination of recycled components and selected minerals crafted through an innovative industrial process.

And guess what?
With 10% silica or less.
