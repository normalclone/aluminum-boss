Cosentino returns to the Milano Design Week and takes part in this outstanding gathering with a full programme of events, introductions and presence at the Salone del Mobile. Cosentino’s agenda will be centered in three main scenarios: a stand at the S.Project Fair (Hall 13 - Stand H12, Rho Fiera Milano) as well as presence throughout Salone via collaborations with other companies; projects highlighted at Fuorisalone; and a complete line-up of activities at Cosentino City Milano.

During these days, Cosentino will introduce the new Dekton collections and the new disruptive nature of Silestone thanks to Hybriq+ technology, as well as the versatility of its materials in kitchens, bathrooms or cladding.
