Versatile resistance

Bring the design you imagine to life

Our strong track record in developing innovative surfaces enables us to offer a broad portfolio of leading brands in the sector. Among them: Silestone and ĒCLOS, two different mineral surfaces with reduced silica content; Dekton, an ultra-compact surface; and Sensa, exclusive natural stones with stain protection.

We offer nearly 500 combinations of colors, finishes, and textures in large-format slabs with multiple thicknesses. This versatility allows us to provide tailored solutions for a wide variety of applications: wall cladding, backsplashes, fireplace surrounds, flooring, staircases, tabletops, and furniture design.

Eco-conscious surfaces for the home

We strive to offer surfaces of the highest quality while contributing to a more sustainable future. We combine cutting-edge technological innovation with exceptional design, utilizing a manufacturing method that prioritizes environmental care.

We incorporate reused components, use only electricity from renewable sources, and recover 99% of the water used, thereby reducing our environmental footprint. Additionally, we reduce and neutralize our CO2 emissions to ensure a positive impact on our surroundings.

Living rooms, bedrooms, or entryways that set trends

With attractive veined designs, refined colors, and elaborated finishes, our surfaces merge the latest trends in design, architecture, and interior design with exceptional solidity and longevity, perfect for any space in your home.

The large format of Silestone and ĒCLOS (up to 128x63 inches), Dekton (up to 130x64 inches), and Sensa (110x71 inches) allows for unrestricted design of any detail, creating greater visual harmony and enhancing the aesthetics of cladding, flooring, and furniture.

Total integration. A modern and harmonious home

The high resistance to stains, scratches and wear of our surfaces (with up to 25 years warranty on Dekton, Silestone and ĒCLOS), combined with our extensive experience in producing large-format pieces and a variety of thicknesses, allows us to offer a wide range of possibilities for interior design.

This technical superiority allows for the creation of functional environments with an integrated and harmonious design, where a single surface can extend from a tabletop to wall cladding, through the exterior of a fireplace, a bed headboard, the floor, or cabinet doors.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS ABOUT KITCHENS

01.

Which Cosentino brands are available for interiors?

The Cosentino brands available for living rooms are Silestone, ĒCLOS, Dekton, Scalea, and Sensa.
02.

Does Cosentino design interiors?

Cosentino specializes in the production and distribution of innovative surfaces for architecture and interior design. While not focusing on interior design directly, its products, such as Silestone, ĒCLOS, Dekton, Sensa, and Scalea, are widely applied in both interior and exterior spaces, including living rooms, kitchens, bathrooms, façades, and more. Cosentino provides a diverse array of colors, textures, and finishes that cater to various styles and design requirements. Locate certified professionals through this link to receive expert advice and bring your vision to life.
03.

How can I apply Cosentino materials in interiors?

Cosentino materials like Silestone, ĒCLOS, Dekton, Sensa, and Scalea can be applied across various living room features, including tables, wall cladding, furniture, flooring, fireplaces, and more. For assistance in applying these materials in your living room, it is advisable to consult with an architectural or interior design expert. Visit the inspiration section on Cosentino's website to explore ideas and examples showcasing the use of these materials in diverse design projects.
04.

Is Dekton heat-resistant?

Dekton is highly heat-resistant and designed to handle hot items such as pots, pans, coffee makers, and heat-emitting electrical appliances. However, we recommend using trivets or other protective layers for extended periods, especially for thicknesses of 4 and 8 mm, and avoiding installation in extremely high-temperature areas such as inside barbecues, ovens, or fireplaces.
