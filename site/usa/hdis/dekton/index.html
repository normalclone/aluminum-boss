DEKTON®

Engineered for exceptional resistance to heat, scratches, stains, UV rays, and wear, making it ideal for both indoor and outdoor applications.

More about Dekton

One Surface. Endless Possibilities. Indoors & Out.

Dekton is a next-generation ultracompact surface created through a high-tech process that mimics the creation of dense natural stone in just 4 hours.

Heated to 2,000 degrees Fahrenheit and pressed under over 25,000 tons of pressure, Dekton slabs are extremely durable, allowing for unique colors and designs that mimic the look and feel of natural stones without the upkeep.

Why Dekton?

Dekton is designed for real life, made to handle heat, sun, spills, scratches, and everyday wear. It transitions seamlessly from indoor to outdoor spaces, delivering a cohesive look throughout the home.

Achieve the highest quality

UV-Resistant

Dekton is highly resistant to ultraviolet (UV) rays and has superior color fastness so colors don’t fade over time, making it the best material for interior and exterior applications.

Scratch-Resistant

Domestic utensils are no match for Dekton, however, we do recommend using a chopping board to protect your utensils.

Stain-resistant

Dekton’s low porosity makes it highly resistant to stains from domestic use and chemical agents, even the toughest wine, coffee, ink, and rust stains can be easily cleaned.

Fire and Heat-Resistant

Dekton withstands the highest temperatures without burning or cracking. Place hot pots and pans safely on the surface with no risk of damage.

Freezing and Thawing-Resistant

Dekton’s properties allow it to go from extreme hot to cold temperatures without risk of damage, perfect for even the coldest environments.

Low maintenance

Dekton does not require sealing or specialized products. Simply wipe with a fresh soapy cloth or sponge and wipe dry with a microfiber cloth.

25-Year Transferable Warranty

The 25-year transferable warranty will bring peace of mind to your renovation project. Warranty is transferable to a new homeowner.

Sustainably Made

A high percentage of Dekton’s contents come from recycled materials, making it an environmentally-friendly choice.

Man-Made Material

Dekton is a man-made surface produced in a high-tech, specialized facility. Consistency in the fabrication and several quality controls assure you the highest satisfaction.
