SILESTONE: FROM QUARTZ COUNTERTOP TO MINERAL HYBRID SURFACES

Mineral Low Silica Surfaces

Silestone with HyBRIQ+: innovative, sustainable, and high-performance mineral surface

Traditional quartz countertops have evolved with Silestone through Hybriq+ Technology. In addition to maintaining all of its benefits such as its colors, textures and high performance, Silestone’s countertops are more sustainable.

COUNTERTOPS THAT ENDURE

SELECTED PREMIUM MINERALS THAT ENSURE HIGH PERFORMANCE

Through our revolutionary Hybriq+® Technology, quartz is replaced with carefully selected premium minerals and recycled materials, reducing the content of crystalline silica in Silestone’s composition, while enhancing its aesthetic attributes and preserving its high resistance and durability of Silestone quartz countertops.

SAFETY AS PART OF SILESTONE’S DNA

Our exclusive Hybriq+® Technology allows us to substitute quartz countertops with new improved surfaces with a maximum of 40% of crystalline silica content, or even as little as only 10% in part of Silestone’s portfolio, while maintaining high performance and excellent finishes

SILESTONE: 25 YEARS LOOKING AS GOOD AS NEW

Through alluring veined patterns, elegant tones or sophisticated finishes, Silestone’s innovative designs and textures combine the latest trends in architecture and interior design with unique strength and durability, specially designed to withstand the daily wear and tear of any kitchen, with up to 25 years of warranty.
