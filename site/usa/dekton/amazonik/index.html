NATURAL BEAUTY. DESIGNED TO ENDURE.

THIS IS NOT WOOD. THIS IS DEKTON®.

Amazonik reinterprets the structure of wood through Dekton’s technical approach, transforming its grain into an ultra compact surface with a natural appearance, enhanced color stability, very low porosity, and resistance to scratches and impacts. These qualities make it suitable for use in wet areas, high traffic zones, and outdoor applications without the need for treatments. The collection is available in two oak inspired tones, Kedar and Zira, suitable for countertops, cladding, and flooring.

Design

NATURAL BEAUTY

Amazonik brings the warmth of oak into a contemporary language. Its high-definition finish adds depth and detail, with a subtle grain that reinforces a natural look and a pleasant touch.

A balance between organic character and innovation.

High performance

DESIGNED TO ENDURE

Created for everyday use, it offers resistance to scratches, impacts and moisture, while its virtually zero porosity makes cleaning and maintenance effortless.

UV stability allows it to be specified outdoors, preserving its appearance over time.

Versatility

MADE FOR EVERY APPLICATION

Available in 8, 12 and 20 mm thicknesses, it adapts to different scales and requirements: visual continuity across worktops and flooring, plank-format solutions, and applications in cladding, furniture and bespoke pieces.

Amazonik opens up creative possibilities for architects and designers, enabling seamless integration across interior and exterior spaces.

Layout Options

Unlimited Flooring Design

Any type of flooring design is possible with Dekton, from large-format continuous surfaces to more dynamic layouts such as herringbone, square, or rectangular patterns in different color combinations. Its versatility offers complete creative freedom, allowing for fully customized configurations tailored to each project.

In addition, although the material is supplied in full slabs, we offer optimized standard cuts that help maximize each piece and make installation easier on site.
